# USPTO Drawing Requirements — Quick Reference Card

## 37 CFR 1.84 — At a Glance

### Paper & Margins
- **Size**: US Letter (8.5x11 in) or A4 — all sheets identical
- **Top margin**: 1 inch (2.5 cm)
- **Left margin**: 1 inch (2.5 cm)
- **Right margin**: 5/8 inch (1.5 cm)
- **Bottom margin**: 3/8 inch (1.0 cm)
- **Usable area**: 6-15/16 x 9-5/8 inches (17.6 x 24.4 cm)
- **NO frames or borders** around drawing area

### Colors
- **Black and white ONLY** (unless color petition granted)
- All strokes: black
- All fills: black, white, or none
- No gradients, no filters, no solid black shading
- Light originates from upper-left corner at 45 degrees

### Text & Reference Numerals
- **Minimum height**: 0.32 cm (1/8 inch, ~9pt)
- **Preferred**: Numerals, not letters
- **English alphabet** (Greek only for math/angles)
- **NO enclosures**: No brackets, circles, quotes around numerals
- **Same part = same numeral** across all views
- Must match specification (every numeral in drawings = in spec, and vice versa)

### Sheet Numbering
- Format: **N/M** (e.g., "1/8", "2/8")
- Position: **Top center, within sight** (not in margin)
- Size: **Larger** than reference characters
- Consecutive Arabic numerals starting with 1

### Figure Numbering
- Format: **FIG. N** (e.g., "FIG. 1", "FIG. 2")
- Independent of sheet numbering
- Size: **Larger** than reference characters
- Consecutive Arabic numerals starting with 1
- **NO** brackets, circles, or quotes

### Lines
- **Durable, clean, black**, uniformly thick
- Heavy enough for adequate reproduction
- Different thicknesses may convey meaning
- Lead lines: short, non-crossing, from numeral to feature

### Shading & Hatching
- Shading: spaced thin lines, light from upper-left at 45 degrees
- Cross-sections: hatching at 45 degrees (oblique parallel lines)
- NO solid black shading (except bar graphs)
- Break hatching around reference numerals

### Scale
- Large enough to read at 2/3 size
- **NO scale notations** ("actual size", "1:2", etc.)

SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

This document describes the reference architecture for an energy-bounded
recursive control system. The system is physically grounded: a spiking
neural network receives environmental sensor input, processes it through
leaky integrate-and-fire neuron dynamics, and produces motor actuation
commands. The motor activity generates mechanical energy that is harvested
through piezoelectric transduction, rectified, and stored in a
supercapacitor. This harvested energy powers the neural network, closing a
self-sustaining energy loop. All computation is constrained by real-time
energy availability derived from the system's own physical interaction with
its environment. The system cannot operate beyond its energy budget. An
energy governor enforces activity-dependent cost (quadratic in spike rate),
ensuring that neural computation remains bounded. A recursive reflection
module feeds the network's prior aggregate output back as input at
configurable gain, providing temporal context for state-dependent
processing. The energy governor and reflection module are bidirectionally
coupled: energy state modulates reflection gain, while reflection-driven
activity influences energy consumption. A cognitive modulation layer applies
attention weighting, intention bias, and contextual gating to the neural
output before it reaches the actuator. When an external advisory system is
available, it provides modulation signals; when disconnected, the system
self-regulates based on energy state alone.


SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS

No claim is made regarding phenomenological consciousness, subjective
experience, qualia, or sentience. The system described herein is an
entirely operational, measurable engineering artifact. All system behavior
is deterministic and fully characterized by explicit mathematical equations
with published validation thresholds. The term "recursive reflection"
refers to a signal feedback mechanism with configurable gain, not to any
form of introspection or awareness. The term "cognitive modulation" refers
to computational parameter adjustment, not to any form of thought or
understanding. Every claim associated with this architecture pertains to
measurable, falsifiable properties of signal processing, energy dynamics,
and control system behavior. The system has been validated through a
rigorous experimental framework comprising a null-hypothesis control
configuration (energy depletes to -4,697 mWh after 2000 steps) and an
alternative-hypothesis experimental configuration (energy grows to +4,170
mWh after 2000 steps), with all validation claims documented as explicit
pass/fail thresholds reproducible via version-controlled source code.
